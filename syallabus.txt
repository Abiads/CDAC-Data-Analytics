1. COURSE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Course Title       : Data Analytics
Programme          : PGCP-AI (Post Graduate Certificate Programme in AI)
Batch              : August 2026
Centre             : ACTS, Pune
Total Duration     : 120 Hours
                     • Theory (T)         : 50 Hours
                     • Lab / Practical (L): 50 Hours
                     • Self-Learning (SL) : 20 Hours

Course Objective   : To introduce students to fundamental and applied concepts 
                     of Data Analytics, statistical modeling, predictive & 
                     prescriptive analytics, and business intelligence.

Prerequisites      : • Knowledge of Python Programming
                     • Fundamentals of Statistics and Mathematics

Evaluation Scheme  : • Theory Examination   : 40%
                     • Lab Examination      : 40%
                     • Internal Assessment  : 20%
                     ---------------------------
                       Total                : 100%

================================================================================
3. SESSION-WISE CURRICULUM BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 1 & 2 | Hours: 4T + 4L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Introduction to Business Analytics using Case Studies
  • Data Analytics Life Cycle: Discovery, Data Preparation, Model Planning
  • Model Building & Implementation
  • Quality Assurance, Documentation, Management Approval
  • Environment Setup and Installation
  • Acceptance and Operation

Lab Assignment:
  • Setup workspace in either Google Colab or Jupyter Notebook.
  • Review documentation and practice basic operations with Pandas, NumPy, 
    and Scikit-learn (sklearn) libraries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 3 | Hours: 2T + 2L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Intelligent Data Analysis and Nature of Data
  • Analytic Processes and Tools
  • Analysis vs. Reporting
  • Modern Data Analytic Tools

Lab Assignment:
  • Explore and analyze the nature of data across various data types.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 4 | Hours: 2T + 2L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Visualization and Exploring Data
  • Case Studies: Making Sound Business Decisions Based on Data

Lab Assignment:
  • Load a sample dataset, explore features, and draw insights.
  • Utilize Matplotlib and Seaborn libraries for data visualization.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 5 | Hours: 2T + 2L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Descriptive Statistical Measures
  • Summary Statistics — Central Tendency & Dispersion:
    - Mean, Median, Mode
    - Quartiles, Percentiles, Range, Interquartile Range (IQR)
    - Variance, Standard Deviation, and Coefficient of Variation

Lab Assignment:
  • Load a dataset and compute the mean, median, mode, and other central 
    tendency and dispersion metrics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 6 & 7 | Hours: 4T + 4L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Sample & Population, Univariate and Bivariate Sampling, Re-sampling
  • Sample Spaces and Events
  • Joint, Conditional, and Marginal Probability
  • Bayes’ Theorem

Lab Assignment:
  • Load a dataset, implement a Naive Bayes classifier, and predict outcomes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 8 | Hours: 2T + 2L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Random Variables
  • Probability Distribution and Data
  • Continuous and Discrete Distributions (Normal, Binomial, Poisson)
  • Central Limit Theorem (CLT)

Lab Assignment:
  • Generate random numbers and test/verify if they follow a normal distribution 
    using SciPy libraries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 9 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Sampling and Estimation
  • Statistical Interfaces

Lab Assignment:
  • Load a dataset and explore sampling techniques.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 10 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Concepts of Correlation
  • Covariance
  • Pearson Correlation
  • Outliers

Lab Assignment:
  • Load a dataset and determine covariance and correlation between fields.
  • Detect and handle outliers in the dataset.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 11 & 12 | Hours: 4T + 4L + 2SL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Hypothesis Testing
  • Z-Test
  • Chi-Square Test
  • Skewness

Lab Assignment:
  • Perform hypothesis testing (Z-test, Chi-square) on sample data and analyze 
    distribution skewness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 13 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Predictive Modelling and Analysis:
    - Applications and Types
    - Benefits and Challenges
    - The Future of Predictive Modelling
    - Limitations of Predictive Modelling
    - Predictive Modelling Tools

Lab Assignment:
  • Set up a predictive modeling pipeline and train baseline models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 14, 15 & 16 | Hours: 6T + 6L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Predictive Modelling (From Correlation to Supervised Segmentation):
    - Identifying Informative Attributes
    - Segmenting Data by Progressive Attributes
    - Model Induction and Prediction
    - Supervised Segmentation
    - Visualizing Segmentations
    - Trees as Sets of Rules
    - Probability Estimation
  • Prescriptive Modelling:
    - Difference between Predictive and Prescriptive Modelling
    - Working Principles of Prescriptive Analytics
    - Practical Examples and Use Cases

Lab Assignment:
  • Build and visualize decision trees; perform supervised segmentation and 
    extract decision rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 17 & 18 | Hours: 4T + 4L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Regression Analysis
  • Forecasting Techniques

Lab Assignment:
  • Implement linear regression models and apply forecasting techniques on 
    datasets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sessions 19 & 20 | Hours: 4T + 4L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Simulation and Risk Analysis
  • Optimization: Linear and Non-linear Optimization

Lab Assignment:
  • Formulate and solve linear optimization problems; run basic simulation 
    models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 21 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Overfitting and Its Avoidance
  • Generalization
  • Holdout Evaluation vs. Cross-Validation

Lab Assignment:
  • Apply cross-validation strategies and compare training vs. validation errors 
    to prevent overfitting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 22 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Decision Analytics
  • Evaluating Classifiers:
    - Analytical Framework
    - Evaluation Metrics & Baselines
    - Performance and Implications for Investments in Data

Lab Assignment:
  • Evaluate classification models using confusion matrices, ROC-AUC, and 
    baseline performance comparisons.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 23 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Evidence and Probabilities
  • Explicit Evidence Combination with Bayes' Rule
  • Probabilistic Reasoning

Lab Assignment:
  • Implement Bayesian evidence updating and probabilistic reasoning models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 24 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Factor Analysis
  • Directional Data Analytics
  • Functional Data Analysis

Lab Assignment:
  • Implement Factor Analysis and dimensionality reduction on multidimensional 
    datasets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Session 25 | Hours: 2T + 2L
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theory:
  • Introduction to Power BI Architecture and Interface
  • Importing and Transforming Data using Power Query
  • Creating Data Visualizations using Charts and Reports
  • Designing Interactive Dashboards and Sharing Insights

Lab Assignment:
  • Build an end-to-end interactive dashboard in Power BI / visualization tool 
    with transformed data, custom charts, and KPI reports.
