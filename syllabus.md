## 3. Session-Wise Curriculum Breakdown

### Module 1: Foundations & Exploratory Data Analysis

#### Sessions 1 & 2: Introduction to Business Analytics & Setup
- **Hours:** 4T + 4L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Introduction to Business Analytics using case studies
  - Data Analytics Life Cycle: Discovery, Data preparation, Model planning
  - Model building implementation
  - Quality assurance, Documentation, Management approval
  - Environment setup and installation
  - Acceptance and operation
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Setup workspace in either Google Colab or Jupyter Notebook.
  - Review documentation and practice basic operations with Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn (sklearn).

#### Session 3: Intelligent Data Analysis & Tools
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Intelligent data analysis and Nature of Data
  - Analytic Processes and Tools
  - Analysis vs. Reporting
  - Modern Data Analytic Tools
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Explore and analyze the nature of data across different types.

#### Session 4: Data Visualization & Business Decisions
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Visualization and Exploring Data
  - Case studies: Making Right Business Decisions based on data
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Load a sample dataset, explore data, and draw insights using Matplotlib and Seaborn libraries.

---

### Module 2: Statistical Methods & Probability Foundations

#### Session 5: Descriptive Statistics
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Descriptive Statistical Measures
  - Summary Statistics: Central Tendency & Dispersion (Mean, Median, Mode, Quartiles, Percentiles, Range, Interquartile Range, Standard Deviation, Variance, and Coefficient of Variation)
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Load a dataset and compute mean, median, mode, IQR, variance, and standard deviation.

#### Sessions 6 & 7: Sampling & Probability Theory
- **Hours:** 4T + 4L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Sample & population, Univariate and bivariate sampling, Re-sampling
  - Sample Spaces and Events
  - Joint, Conditional, and Marginal Probability
  - Bayes’ Theorem
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Load a dataset, implement Naive Bayes classification, and predict outcomes.

#### Session 8: Random Variables & Probability Distributions
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Random Variables
  - Probability Distribution and Data
  - Continuous and discrete distributions (Normal, Binomial, and Poisson distributions)
  - Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Generate random numbers and test for normal distribution using SciPy libraries.

#### Session 9: Sampling & Estimation
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Sampling and Estimation
  - Statistical Interfaces
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Load a dataset and experiment with sampling techniques.

#### Session 10: Correlation, Covariance & Outlier Analysis
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Concepts of Correlation
  - Covariance
  - Pearson Correlation
  - Outliers detection and treatment
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Compute covariance and correlation matrices between features. Detect and treat outliers in the data.

#### Sessions 11 & 12: Hypothesis Testing
- **Hours:** 4T + 4L + 2SL
- **Theory:**
  - Hypothesis Testing concepts & significance levels
  - Z-Test
  - Chi-Square Test
  - Skewness and distribution analysis
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Conduct hypothesis testing (Z-test, Chi-square) on sample data; evaluate skewness.

---

### Module 3: Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics

#### Session 13: Predictive Modelling Fundamentals
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Predictive modelling and analysis
  - Applications and types
  - Benefits and challenges
  - The Future and limitations of predictive modelling
  - Predictive modelling tools
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Build baseline predictive models in Scikit-learn.

#### Sessions 14, 15 & 16: Supervised Segmentation & Decision Trees
- **Hours:** 6T + 6L
- **Theory:**
  - **Predictive Modelling (Correlation to Supervised Segmentation):**
    - Identifying Informative Attributes
    - Segmenting Data by Progressive Attributes
    - Models Induction and Prediction
    - Supervised Segmentation & Visualizing Segmentations
    - Decision Trees as Sets of Rules
    - Probability Estimation
  - **Prescriptive Modelling:**
    - Difference between predictive and prescriptive modelling
    - Working mechanisms of prescriptive analytics
    - Practical examples and industry use cases
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Train and visualize decision trees; perform supervised segmentation and extract decision rule-sets.

#### Sessions 17 & 18: Regression & Forecasting
- **Hours:** 4T + 4L
- **Theory:**
  - Regression Analysis (Simple and Multiple Linear Regression)
  - Forecasting Techniques
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Fit regression models, analyze residuals and $R^2$, and perform forecasting.

#### Sessions 19 & 20: Simulation & Optimization
- **Hours:** 4T + 4L
- **Theory:**
  - Simulation and Risk Analysis (Monte Carlo Simulation)
  - Optimization: Linear and Non-linear Optimization
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Formulate and solve linear optimization problems; perform simulation runs.

---

### Module 4: Model Evaluation, Advanced Analytics & BI

#### Session 21: Model Validation & Generalization
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Overfitting and its avoidance
  - Generalization principles
  - Holdout Evaluation vs. Cross-Validation
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Implement K-Fold cross-validation and evaluate train/test performance curves.

#### Session 22: Decision Analytics & Classifier Evaluation
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Decision Analytics
  - Evaluating Classifiers:
    - Analytical Framework
    - Evaluation metrics (Confusion Matrix, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC)
    - Baseline models
    - Performance and economic implications for investments in data
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Evaluate classifiers using comprehensive metrics, ROC-AUC curves, and cost-benefit tradeoffs.

#### Session 23: Evidence & Probabilistic Reasoning
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Evidence and Probabilities
  - Explicit Evidence Combination with Bayes' Rule
  - Probabilistic Reasoning
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Implement Bayesian inference and probability updating algorithms.

#### Session 24: Factor Analysis & Advanced Analytics
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Factor Analysis
  - Directional Data Analytics
  - Functional Data Analysis
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Perform Factor Analysis and dimensionality reduction on high-dimensional data.

#### Session 25: Business Intelligence with Power BI
- **Hours:** 2T + 2L
- **Theory:**
  - Introduction to Power BI architecture and interface
  - Importing and transforming data using Power Query
  - Creating data visualizations using charts and reports
  - Designing interactive dashboards and sharing insights
- **Lab Assignment:**
  - Create an end-to-end interactive dashboard in Power BI incorporating Power Query data transformation, KPI cards, and charts.
